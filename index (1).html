My Projects

Finance Management Application

Hackathon Project

A finance-related application designed to help users manage and understand their spending. Users provide information about themselves, including their financial goals, and the app keeps track of their expenses.

Based on the user's information and goals, the application suggests:

  • Where the user should spend
  • Where the user should avoid spending
  • How their spending relates to their personal goals

The goal of the application is to help users make better spending decisions according to their individual financial goals. Ritul developed this as part of a hackathon, gaining experience working on a practical idea in a collaborative, time-limited environment.
