About Me

I chose Computer Science because I've always been fascinated by understanding how things actually work behind the scenes. I'm not satisfied with just using a website or an app — I want to know what's happening underneath it, how the pieces connect, and why it behaves the way it does.

That curiosity naturally pulled me toward mechanisms and backend concepts, rather than just the surface of technology. I enjoy thinking about how websites and systems work internally, tracing the logic from a click all the way down to the code that makes it happen.

Alongside that curiosity, I consider myself imaginative — a bit of a daydreamer. I picture ideas and possibilities in my head, and what excites me most is the process of turning that imagination into something real, functional, and useful through code.

Right now, I'm still early in that process: curious, learning, experimenting, and growing one project at a time.

Finance Management Application

A finance-related application designed to help users manage and understand their spending. Users provide information about themselves — including financial goals and other relevant details — and the app keeps track of their expenses.

Based on that information and each user's goals, the application offers suggestions about where to spend, where to avoid spending, and how spending choices relate back to personal goals — with the aim of helping people make better spending decisions.

Ritul built this as part of a hackathon, giving him hands-on experience developing a practical idea in a collaborative, time-limited environment.
